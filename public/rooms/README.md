# Drop the six room plates here

Exact filenames. The stage keys them off the scene id, so the name is the room.

    coffee.webp      campus cafe        two chairs, one small table, nothing else happening
    mission.webp     campus bookstore   narrow aisle, crowded shelves, one gap where a book was
    gallery.webp     student gallery    white wall, hung work out of focus, polished floor
    postshow.webp    theatre -> tacos   wet pavement at night, lit doorway behind
    group.webp       small group event  six chairs in a loose ring, one pulled slightly out
    study.webp       library steps      wide stone steps at dusk, strip-lit doorway above

1600 x 1000, WebP, under 180 KB each. Dark and low contrast -- these sit behind
white text at 18% opacity and must not fight it.

EVERY ROOM IS EMPTY. No people, no figures, no silhouettes, no reflections of
people. An empty room waiting is the thesis, and it also keeps the whole project
clear of depicting anybody.

No readable text, no signage, no logos.

Nothing else to do. Drop a file in and it appears; delete it and the stage is
exactly what it is today. Prompts are in ../../docs/IMAGE-BRIEF.md.
